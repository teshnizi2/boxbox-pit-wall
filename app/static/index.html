How to play

  1. 1
    Pick a driverYou are his pit wall. Your job: decide when he stops for new tyres.
  2. 2
    Press Start the raceThe real race replays lap by lap. Every lap our AI (Jev) says BOX or STAY OUT.
  3. 3
    Press BOX, pick a tyreOld tyres get slow. Use at least two different tyres. Finish ahead of the real team to win.

Pick your strategist

Strategist for Max Verstappen

During the race: press B (or the BOX button) to pit, then S, M or H for Soft, Medium or Hard tyres. Space pauses.

    How it works

    Esc or H to close
    1

    The data lives in Elastic

    Every lap of the real 2024 Dutch Grand Prix is stored here: 2.9 million measurements and 3,531 laps. Think of it as a library with all the race books.

    Elastic Serverless · ES|QL
    2

    Hopsworks turns it into numbers and models

    It works out how fast tyres wear and that a pit stop costs 24 seconds. Two small models learn from this: one guesses lap times (only 0.47 s off), one guesses “should he pit now?” (90% right).

    Feature store · lap_features · AUC 0.90
    3

    Jev gives advice every lap

    Jev is an AI co-pilot. Each lap it says BOX or STAY OUT and how sure it is, like a percentage on a radio call.

    TypeSafe Jev · about 0.4 s
    4

    Claude writes the radio

    Claude writes the team radio messages and the debrief after the race, using the data in Elastic. The game puts it all together and replays the race lap by lap.

    Claude via Elastic · Agent Builder · Kibana
